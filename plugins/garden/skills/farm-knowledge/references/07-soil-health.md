# Regenerative agriculture and soil health

No legal definition; an outcome-based approach to rebuilding organic matter, biodiversity and water cycling while
staying profitable.

## Principles
- **NRCS soil health:** maximise cover, minimise disturbance, maximise biodiversity, keep living roots; many add
  integrate livestock.
- **Gabe Brown** (*Dirt to Soil*, 2018): know your context, armour the soil, minimise disturbance, diversity, living
  roots year-round, integrate animals.

## Key people and ideas
- **Elaine Ingham:** soil food web; fungal:bacterial ratio matched to succession (annuals bacterial, trees fungal).
- **Christine Jones:** liquid carbon pathway (root exudates to mycorrhizae build stable carbon); diverse covers beat
  monocultures; excess soluble N and P switch the trade off.
- **Walter Jehne, Didi Pershouse:** the soil carbon sponge; infiltration and water-holding first.
- **Ray Archuleta:** slake and infiltration demos. **Nicole Masters** (*For the Love of Soil*): brix and infiltration
  monitoring. **John Kempf:** plant health pyramid, sap analysis. **Albrecht / Gary Zimmer:** base-saturation
  balancing. **Rick Haney:** Haney soil health test.

## Practices
- **Compost:** hot aerobic 55-65 °C, C:N ~25-30:1; Johnson-Su bioreactor for fungal static compost; vermicompost.
- **Cover crops:** in the desert, winter-rain mixes only (oats, barley, cereal rye, fava, Austrian pea, vetch,
  mustard, phacelia), sown Oct-Nov. SARE *Managing Cover Crops Profitably*.
- **Mulch** (free wood chips via ChipDrop or arborists), **biochar** (charge with compost or urine first; helps sand
  hold water and nutrients), no-till, keyline subsoiling, animal impact, biological inputs (`04-knf-jadam.md`).
- **Monitoring:** infiltration rings (time for 1 in), slake test, ground cover %, organic matter, earthworm and
  arthropod counts, brix, photo points.

## Soil in the high desert
- Expect alluvial sandy loams and loamy sands from granitic San Gabriel fan material, OM under 1%, pH 7.5-8.5,
  possible caliche, possible salinity or sodicity in low spots.
- **Test before acting:** texture, pH, EC, SAR/ESP, CEC, OM, nitrate, Olsen P, K, Ca, Mg, Na, B, Fe, Zn, Mn. Labs:
  UC Davis ANR Analytical Lab, Ward, A&L Western, Logan Labs, Haney test labs.
- Organic matter builds slowly where water limits carbon: concentrate compost and chips in basins and rows, don't
  broadcast.
- Iron chlorosis at high pH: acidifying organic matter, chelated iron if needed, tolerant rootstocks.
- Test the well water for EC, sodium, boron and bicarbonate; high bicarbonate raises pH and clogs drip.
- Gypsum fixes sodic soils (not saline ones); leaching fixes salinity only where drainage exists.
