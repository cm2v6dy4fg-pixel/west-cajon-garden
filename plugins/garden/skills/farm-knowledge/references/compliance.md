# Rules, permits and paperwork (California / San Bernardino County)

Researched October 2026 from public sources. Rules and fees change: confirm with the agency before spending money,
and say so when answering.

## Groundwater (Mojave Water Agency)
- Phelan is in the Mojave Basin adjudication. Under 10 acre-feet/yr (~3.26M gal) is a **Minimal Producer**.
- Wells producing since 1 Jul 2022 pay a per-acre-foot Minimal Producers Fee on the tax bill (MWA Ordinance 14).
  Over 10 AF/yr joins the court case as a party. Water must be used on the parcel.
- A new well needs an MWA **Notice of Intent** 15 days before drilling ($500 fine if not).
- Mature farm demand is ~0.7 AF/yr, but the existing well is weak; plan on hauled water until a drawdown test
  proves yield.

## Zoning (County Development Code 84.04, RL zone)
- **Hogs:** 1 per 20,000 sq ft, max 9, as a primary use only (not accessory). Confirm with Land Use Services that
  pigs count as a primary use of the lot before buying stock.
- **Roosters:** 1 acre required, max 9. **Sheep/goats:** 1 per 4,000 sq ft (primary use).
- **Pens:** 70 ft from neighbours' homes, 100 ft from domestic wells.
- RL density 1 unit / 2.5 ac, 20% lot coverage (confirm desert-region values).
- On-site sales and agritourism: check the land-use tables with Land Use Services.

## Meat
- Sell whole/half/quarter **live-animal shares** under custom-exempt rules: the customer buys the live animal and pays
  the processor directly. Price per head or by live weight, **never carcass weight**. Live-weight pricing needs a
  weighmaster licence and a sealed scale.
- Retail cuts need USDA-inspected slaughter. Mobile slaughter of hogs needs no state registration.
- Poultry: federal producer/grower exemption up to 1,000 birds/yr; check CDFA Meat, Poultry and Egg Safety before
  selling.

## Eggs
- **CDFA egg handler registration** (Egg Safety and Quality Management): $75 to start, then $50/yr (CCR Title 3
  §1358.3). Report changes within 30 days. Duck and quail eggs are exempt.
- Keep eggs at **45 °F or colder** in storage and transport. Sell only clean, uncracked, Grade B or better.
- **Carton label:** farm name, address and ZIP; quantity; CDFA handler code; "CA SEFS Compliant" at least 1/4 in tall;
  size and grade if claimed.
- **Cage-free** housing required for all eggs sold in California (Prop 12); 116 sq in per bird for flocks of 9+.
- Keep sales records 3 years (date, quantity, quality, buyer; buyer names not needed for direct consumer sales).
- **Small-flock exemption:** 500 hens or fewer, selling direct to household consumers without advertising at the
  farm: no size, grade or name/address marks needed. CDFA registration still required.

| Route | What you need | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Farm stand (Community Food Producer, AB 1990) | County Environmental Health Community Food Producer registration (no fee at present) + CDFA registration | Up to 15 dozen a month; may be unrefrigerated at your own stand; sell to the public, permitted restaurants and cottage food operators; deliver in person |
| Certified farmers' markets | Certified Producer Certificate from the county Agricultural Commissioner + CDFA registration | Market may allow unrefrigerated own eggs out of sun, labelled "refrigerate after purchase" with pack date; refrigerate unsold eggs after 4 days |
| Grocery and larger accounts | CDFA registration + full labelling | Over 3,000 hens, the FDA Egg Safety Rule applies |

Guide: UC ANR *How to Sell Eggs in California* (2017, predates Prop 12).

## Other enterprises
- **Cottage food** (county Environmental Health): Class A direct (~$75k cap, inflation-adjusted), Class B indirect
  (~$150k). Jams, dried fruit and dried tea blends are allowed; syrups (including elderberry) only if on the CDPH
  approved list.
- **Apiary:** annual registration with the county agricultural commissioner.
- **Nursery:** CDFA nursery stock licence + county inspection + seller's permit before selling plants.
- **Fish:** CDFW aquaculture registration; most tilapia restricted.
- **Business:** CDTFA seller's permit (and the partial sales-tax exemption on farm equipment), county business
  licence. Tax: Schedule F; keep records that pass the IRS for-profit factors (hobby-loss rules).

## Earthworks and water
- County grading permit for earthworks. Work in or diverting from the wash may need a CDFW §1602 Lake or Streambed
  Alteration Agreement and possibly Regional Water Board involvement.
- Rooftop rainwater capture needs no water right (Rainwater Capture Act 2012).
- Laundry-to-landscape greywater needs no permit if it meets CPC Ch. 15; check onsite wastewater rules on septic.

## EQIP cost-share paperwork (NRCS)
- Applications are taken all year; to be ranked for a fiscal year, apply before California's cutoff (FY2026 was
  15 Jan 2026; plan on mid-January 2027 and confirm).
- **Never start work before the contract is signed:** nothing installed earlier is paid, including trees, drip and mulch.
- Office: USDA Victorville Service Center (NRCS and FSA), 15415 W. Sand St., Suite 103, Victorville; 760-843-6882.
- **Step 1, FSA (one visit):** AD-2047 customer data worksheet; farm and tract number (needs the deed); CCC-902I farm
  operating plan; CCC-941 AGI certification; AD-1026 HEL/wetland certification; **CCC-860 Part C beginning farmer**
  (under 10 years farming; unlocks higher rates and up to 50% paid in advance); SF-1199A (NRCS) and SF-3881 (FSA)
  direct deposit. Bring photo ID, SSN or EIN, grant deed, a voided check and the last tax return.
- **Step 2, NRCS-CPA-1200:** program EQIP; tick Beginning Farmer; land control owner; crops orchard/tree, nursery,
  vegetables; livestock swine, bees, poultry.
- Practices to raise: 380 windbreak, 612 tree/shrub establishment, 441 micro-irrigation, 484 mulching, 311 alley
  cropping, 325 high tunnel (nursery), 381 silvopasture if animals graze under trees. Ask for the current California
  payment schedule.
- After applying: planner visit and conservation plan → ranking after the cutoff → contract (request the advance
  then) → install to NRCS standards → inspection → payment.
- **CDFA Healthy Soils** is closed to individual farmers in 2026; producer funding is expected in 2027 through
  partner organizations (ask the Mojave Desert RCD).

## Manufactured home
Finish the HCD 433A Notice of Installation on a permanent foundation (county Building & Safety permit, then HCD
filing) before FSA or other land-backed financing, since lenders treat personal and real property differently.
