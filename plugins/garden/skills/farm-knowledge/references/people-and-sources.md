# People, communities and sources

## Free documents worth having
- P.A. Yeomans, *The Challenge of Landscape* (Yeomans Plow Co PDF).
- SARE: *Building Soils for Better Crops*, *Managing Cover Crops Profitably*, *Manage Insects on Your Farm*, *Crop
  Rotation on Organic Farms*, *Building a Sustainable Business*, *Training Manual for Applied Agroforestry Practices*.
- University of Hawai'i CTAHR KNF sheets: BIO-9 (IMO), SA-7 FPJ, SA-8 LAB, SA-9 DSW, SA-10 WCA, SA-11 OHN, SA-12 FAA,
  LM-23 natural farming swine deep litter.
- SARE FNE22-001 (farm vs commercial inoculants); Keli'ikuli 2018 UH thesis (KNF bacteria).
- *JADAM Organic Farming* (free English PDF via UNDP IDEASS).
- USDA National Agroforestry Center notes; USDA FS GTR WO-96.
- Lancaster, *Eight Rainwater Harvesting Principles*; Quivira Coalition / Zeedyk *Erosion Control Field Guide*;
  USFS RMRS-GTR-272; Utah State *Low-Tech Process-Based Restoration of Riverscapes* manual.
- University of Arizona AZ1937 (seed-ball design); UNLV 2025 *Reevaluating Desert Upland Restoration Sites*.
- University of Nevada, Reno *Fruit Trees for Southern Nevada 2024-25*.
- Mojave Water Agency Ordinance 14 and Notice of Intent to Extract Water; CDFA Healthy Soils practice list.
- Oregon State open textbook *Introduction to Permaculture*.
- J. Russell Smith, *Tree Crops* (1929; public domain, archive.org).

The owner keeps downloaded copies of these (and the project's own plans and design manuals) in the farm project
files, sorted by topic.

## Books to buy
Mollison *Permaculture: A Designers' Manual*; Holmgren *Principles and Pathways*; Lancaster *Rainwater Harvesting for
Drylands and Beyond*; Yeomans *Water for Every Farm*; Shepard *Restoration Agriculture* and *Water for Any Farm*;
Doherty *Regrarians Handbook* (eBook chapters); Fukuoka *The One-Straw Revolution* and *Sowing Seeds in the Desert*;
Zeedyk & Clothier *Let the Water Do the Work*; Ludwig *Create an Oasis with Greywater*; Perkins *Regenerative
Agriculture*; Salatin *Polyface Micro*; Savory *Holistic Management*; Palmer *The Regenerative Grower's Guide to
Garden Amendments*; Masters *For the Love of Soil*; Brown *Dirt to Soil*.
