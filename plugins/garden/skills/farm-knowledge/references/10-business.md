# Farm business, economics and self-sufficiency

The goal (owner's decision, 2026-10-11): the farm feeds the household first and covers the household's current
bills, on 4-16 hours a week. Groceries not bought count as income. Track the bills the farm covers once a quarter;
the target is met when that equals the full monthly bill total.

## Mindset from the core authors
- **Mark Shepard:** most farms don't pay all the bills; lower the cost of production; perennials amortise; avoid
  input-dependent profit schemes; value-add and diversify.
- **Joel Salatin:** stack enterprises, cheap portable infrastructure, sell direct, start with fast cash-flow
  enterprises (eggs, broilers) before slow ones (trees).
- **Richard Perkins** (Ridgedale, *Regenerative Agriculture*, 2019): every enterprise must pass a financial test;
  systems and time-and-motion matter as much as ecology; education is a large share of revenue.
- **Jean-Martin Fortier, Curtis Stone:** high revenue per bed-foot, but both depend on cheap water.
- **Ben Hartman** (*The Lean Farm*): cut waste in motion, inventory and overproduction.
- **Allan Savory:** Income − Profit = Expenses; gross profit per enterprise; marginal reaction.

## Evaluating enterprises
1. **Gross margin:** revenue − direct variable costs (feed, seed, packaging, processing, water).
2. **Rank by the scarcest resources:** water first, then labour, capital, land. Net $ per 1,000 gal and net $ per
   labour hour. Plan figures per 1,000 gal: pork ~$621, nursery ~$393, jujube ~$53, pomegranate ~$50, stone fruit
   $9-13. At $60/1,000 gal hauled, anything below ~$60 loses money on hauled water.
3. **Hours:** rough weekly hours from the plan review: laying hens 1-2; a pig batch of up to 9 on deep litter 2-4
   while on the farm; trees on drip about 1 in season. That leaves room for one income line at a time.
4. **Cash-flow timing:** fast (eggs, broilers, nursery cuttings, honey/nucs, courses) vs slow (trees, vines, asparagus).
5. **Holistic testing questions** (`09-livestock-bees.md`) before any capital purchase.
6. **Break-even and payback** for infrastructure (tanks, greenhouse, well pump).

## Self-sufficiency (resources)
- **Water:** roof + well + tanks + soil storage; cut demand with mulch, shade, drip and timing.
- **Food:** plan's food garden ~$8,700/yr grocery offset at maturity; plus eggs, pork, honey, winter greens.
- **Energy:** solar is already contracted for the house; add a DC solar pump for irrigation, a passive solar
  greenhouse, wood from prunings.
- **Fertility:** pig and poultry deep litter, compost, KNF inputs, cover crops, free wood chips.
- **Plants:** propagate everything from on-site stock; it saves money and seeds the nursery.

## Income lines, in order of fit
Primary production alone nets about $17.6-31.6k/yr at maturity. Start with the lines that need the fewest hours
per dollar and no fixed hours (eggs, pig shares, nursery stock from cuttings); add design-install work and
workshops when day-job hours can drop.
1. **Nursery** (CDFA licence, county inspection, seller's permit): desert-adapted fruit (pomegranate, fig, grape,
   jujube, mulberry) and natives (mesquite, desert willow, elderberry, saltbush) for restoration, HOA and landscape
   buyers. Very high $ per gallon on a small footprint.
2. **Water-harvesting and keyline design-install services** in Phelan, Piñon Hills, Oak Hills, Hesperia: tanks,
   greywater, earthworks, windbreaks. The farm is the showroom. Potentially the largest line.
3. **Education:** workshops (grafting, propagation, KNF, water harvesting, bees, pigs), tours, online content.
   Agritourism needs county approval and liability insurance.
4. **Value-added:** honey, dried elderberry and tea (cottage food), dried jujube and pomegranate, jams.
5. **Livestock:** pork shares, eggs, broilers, nucs and queens.
6. **Farm stays** if zoning allows.
Defer aquaponics (`plan-changes.md` #4).

## Funding and finance
- **USDA FSA:** microloans (up to $50k), operating loans, beginning farmer down-payment and guaranteed loans.
- **NRCS EQIP:** cost-share on windbreaks, tree establishment, micro-irrigation, high tunnels, water harvesting,
  mulching; beginning farmer rates and advance payment. Paperwork: `compliance.md`.
- **CDFA Healthy Soils** and **SWEEP** when open; **Western SARE** farmer/rancher grants for on-farm trials (e.g. KNF
  deep-litter pigs or microcatchments in the high desert); **USDA Value-Added Producer Grants**; **REAP** for solar
  pumping; Mojave Desert RCD.
